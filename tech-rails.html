High-Velocity Rails

Ballastless monolithic slab tracks, elastic vibration-damping fasteners, and precision alignment metrics.

1. Engineering Purpose & Speed Parameters

High-speed rail infrastructure demands track alignment networks that maintain absolute geometric precision under extreme dynamic forces. Standard gravel ballast tracks shift and warp rapidly when trains exceed speeds of 250 km/h, introducing intense maintenance requirements. High-velocity rail tech bypasses these physical limits by using solid, engineered slab structures to lock the tracks in place permanently.

2. Monolithic Ballastless Slab Installation

RAYTON implements an advanced ballastless concrete slab track system. The sub-base is poured as a perfectly level, continuous reinforced concrete foundation mat. Precision alignment machines then position the heavy steel rails with millimeter accuracy before a secondary locking layer of high-strength concrete grout is distributed around the base frames, forming an unyielding, solid track bed.

3. Elastic Vibration Fasteners

To safely handle the intense mechanical rumble of high-velocity transit, the steel rails are held down using highly flexible, elastic fastening assemblies. These specialized clips incorporate thick rubberized dampening pads that absorb rolling vibrations, preventing shockwaves from fracturing the concrete slabs while providing a smooth, completely stable passenger ride.

4. Thermal Expansion Control Loops

Continuous steel rails expand and contract significantly across seasonal temperature changes. Our transit teams install long, continuously welded rails (CWR) paired with hydraulic tension-adjusting expansion joints at specific intervals. This layout lets the steel breathe naturally under intense summer heat without warping out of alignment, ensuring trains can safely maintain a 350 km/h velocity.

System Parameters

  • Track Framework: Ballastless Concrete Slab
  • Speed Threshold: 350 km/h Continuous
  • Maintenance Cost: -80% Structural Drain
  • Joint Class: Continuously Welded Rail
  • Vibration Control: Elastic Fastener Clips
  • Gauge Deviation: Less than 1mm Tolerance
