High-End Finishes & Framing

Load-bearing high-capacity composite column layouts, long-span post-tension slabs, and high-performance acoustics.

1. Engineering Purpose & Column-Free Layouts

Luxury residential design demands completely open interior layouts with clean sightlines and expansive double-height volumes. Traditional construction ruins this look by dropping structural columns directly into center living areas. RAYTON fixes this issue by shifting the entire vertical dead load outward to the perimeter wall shell using high-capacity composite columns containing ultra-dense concrete and structural steel cores.

2. Long-Span Post-Tension Residential Slabs

To bridge the massive, wide distances between these outer load-bearing columns, we cast thin, long-span post-tension concrete floor slabs. High-strength steel cables threaded inside the slab forms are pulled tight under immense hydraulic force once the concrete hardens. This internal compression keeps the floor incredibly thin and rigid, preventing any sagging or flexing and allowing us to build expansive open living rooms without center supports.

3. Acoustic Vibration Dampening Layers

Multi-family luxury builds must maintain absolute acoustic isolation between units. Beneath premium hardwood or stone floor surfaces, we install an multi-layered elastomeric acoustic membrane sheet. This sound-shield decouples the flooring material from the structural concrete slab, absorbing high-frequency footstep impacts and structural vibrations to keep noise transmission well below premium quiet-zone limits.

4. Advanced Architectural Facing Materials

Visible surfaces use top-tier composite stone matrices and architectural metals chosen for both beauty and weathering resilience. These external claddings are mounted using heavy-duty hidden steel brackets rather than standard grouting. This creates a floating structural air gap behind the paneling that keeps interior moisture out and cuts building temperature shifts year-round.
