Pre-Stressed Concrete Systems

High-performance composite material architecture, hydraulic tensioning mechanics, and wide-span element design.

1. Engineering Purpose & Internal Mechanics

Standard structural concrete possesses incredible compressive strength but is naturally brittle under tension (stretching and bending forces). Pre-stressed concrete systems solve this structural limitation by introducing calculated internal compressive loads during the manufacturing stage. This preset compression balances out the external stretching forces the material faces once placed into active service under heavy vehicle traffic or structural loads.

2. The Tensioning Execution Workflow

RAYTON utilizes two distinct methods depending on the asset envelope: Pre-Tensioning and Post-Tensioning. In pre-tensioning, high-tensile steel cables (tendons) are threaded through structural steel forms and pulled to extreme force limits using synchronized hydraulic jack systems before the concrete is poured. In post-tensioning, the steel tendons are jacked tightly *after* the concrete has achieved 75% of its certified curing strength, anchored firmly using specialized steel wedge blocks.

3. Wide-Span Girder Optimization

When the internal steel tendons are released or locked into place, they transfer their massive tensile energy into the surrounding concrete, slightly curving the girder upward. When heavy vehicles cross the span, the girder deflects flat, keeping the material entirely under compression. This eliminates traditional cracking risks and allows us to deploy mega-girders across wide rivers and highways with minimal column supports.

4. Lifecycle Durability & Environmental Defense

Because the internal compression keeps micro-fissures tightly closed, moisture, salts, and chemical pollutants cannot seep in to rust the steel rebar inside. This intense material sealing shield gives RAYTON’s transit assets a certified operational lifespan exceeding a full century under harsh seasonal changes.
